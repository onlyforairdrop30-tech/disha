About Our School

Samarpan Public School

Samarpan Public School is a completely secular institution that instills among its students utmost respect for our rich cultural heritage and a deep sense of regard for the values upheld by our Constitution.

Managed by Samarpan Education & Research Foundation, dedicated to providing quality education and fostering all-round personality development through modern infrastructure and experienced faculties.

  • CBSE & GSEB Board Affiliation
  • Smart Classrooms with Digital Boards
  • 1:15 Teacher-Student Ratio
  • STEM, Sports & Cultural Activities

Our Campus & Facilities

Smart Classrooms

Modern classrooms with digital interactive smart boards and engaging multimedia learning tools.

Science Labs

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories for hands-on scientific experiments.

Computer Lab

High-tech computer lab with high-speed internet and modern software for digital literacy.

Library

Vast collection of encyclopedias, reference books, journals, fiction, and digital resources.

Sports & Yoga

Expansive sports grounds for cricket, football, yoga sessions, athletics, and indoor games.

Safe Transport

Safe and verified school bus fleet covering all major routes across Gandhinagar with GPS tracking.
